# Research 011 — Collective phase, agent coarse-graining and a falsifiable memory hypothesis

## 011-A: Exact projective identity and null moments
Let N classical phases theta_j define z_j=exp(i theta_j)/sqrt(N), a unit vector on S^(2N−1), and u=(1,...,1)/sqrt(N). The Kuramoto order R=|sum exp(i theta_j)/N| satisfies F=R²=|u†z|². Fubini–Study distance is arccos R. A shared phase shift is the Hopf U(1) action; neither F nor the projective ray changes. This is an embedding of classical phase data in projective space, not a quantum brain claim. For iid uniform phases E|sum exp(i theta)|²=N; E|sum exp(i theta)|⁴=2N²−N by matching indices in the expansion. Thus E F=1/N and Var F=(N−1)/N³. Dependence and nonuniform marginal phase invalidate this null.

## 011-B: Nonidentifiability by synchrony
Simulate Euler–Maruyama with dt=.02 s in a rotating frame: dtheta_j=[omega_j+K Im(Z exp(−i theta_j))+H sin(−theta_j)]dt+sqrt(2D)dW_j, Z=mean exp(i theta). Independent Gaussian detunings (standard deviation 1 rad/s); initial uniform phases; independent noise. Match initial conditions and noise across three simulations: (K,0), (0,H), (0,0). At any instant the drift depends on the complex field KZ+H; a field chosen to equal KZ would reproduce the instantaneous coupling drift without revealing its origin. An independent shared drive need not mimic the full joint process; nevertheless high R or PLV can occur without interaction. Pairwise PLV=|mean exp(i(theta_i−theta_j))|, not wPLI or causal connectivity. Report last-half means; samples are correlated, so no significance is assigned. Seeded simulations are not human EEG or proof of physical nonlocality. No flashing or audio stimulation.

## 011-C: A precise agent-composition obstruction
Inspired by Hoffman et al.'s stochastic kernels, consider states (x,h) in {0,1}², visible flip probability p_h, hidden flip q=.1, and stay probability 1−p_h−q. The symmetric stochastic matrix has uniform stationary law (not necessarily unique at endpoints). Partition by visible bit x. Strong lumpability (all initial distributions) requires equal transition mass to each target block for any two states in a block: exactly p0=p1. The defect is |p0−p1|. Conditional predictive information in bits under the uniform law is I(X_next;H|X)=h2((p0+p1)/2)−[h2(p0)+h2(p1)]/2, strictly positive when p0≠p1 by strict concavity. This is a test of Markov state reduction, not a claim to measure qualia, fusion, IIT or phenomenal consciousness. A non-lumpable projection may require history or hidden state; it is not an autonomous Markov chain for every initialization.

## 011-D: Operational morphic hypothesis (speculative)
A narrow testable surrogate for similarity-dependent historical influence: logit P(Y=1|pair,target)=alpha_pair+beta*similarity*dose; sham logit=alpha_pair. Synthetic alpha_pair=−.5+N(0,1), independent Bernoulli draws conditional on pair. The null beta=0. Independent pairs; matched nuisance; two-sided exact conditional McNemar test: if a=target-only successes and b=sham-only successes, under exchangeability a|a+b~Binomial(a+b,1/2). p=min(1,2*P(Binomial<=min(a,b))). Monte Carlo at fixed seed estimates rejection rates; 300 repetitions are illustrative. This model does not implement a physical field and a positive simulated beta is inserted, not discovered.

Empirical protocol: randomly assign matched novel patterns to prior training or sham at a remote site; counterbalance stimulus identities and match difficulty; lock randomization and hash stimuli before data collection; prevent communication, timing cues and shared-device leakage; blind receivers, assessors and analysts; measure a single prespecified learning outcome in independent receiver pairs; stratify by site and batch (if exposures shared, randomize/analyze at exposure-batch level rather than treating receivers as independent); preregister effect size, sample size, exclusions, stopping and multiplicity; replicate independently with concealed assignments. A null result bounds the tested effect, not all metaphysical possibilities. A positive result requires ordinary leakage and confound audit before attributing it to nonlocal memory.

## Empirical EEG path (not yet performed)
Use dual-EEG joint agency or triadic social decision datasets. Inspect event alignment and consent/license; use predeclared band and trial windows, artifact removal, reference sensitivity, event-matched pseudo-dyads, stimulus regression and block-level permutation respecting dependence. Compare PLV with lag-sensitive measures such as wPLI; neither proves direct causality. Held-out data and independent replication. These are ordinary interaction datasets, not direct tests of morphic resonance.
