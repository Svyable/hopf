03 / INFORMATION-THEORETIC BOUND

How much information is the future worth?

Expected log evidence is a difference of binary Kullback–Leibler divergences. The oracle attains the maximum when its forecast equals the true conditional target probability.

When p is the actual marginal conditional on the available history, averaging the oracle term over the added pre-target features equals conditional mutual information. A mismatch in p can instead reflect ordinary predictability.

04 / REPRODUCIBLE EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN

Lock the past before drawing the future.

01 · COMMIT

Freeze the score

Preregister the causal feature extraction, time window, prediction model and stake mixture. Append an independently timestamped commitment before any target is drawn.

02 · RANDOMIZE

Generate the event

Draw a target from an audited independent randomizer after the commitment. Record exact conditional probabilities and randomization records; no target-aware exclusions.

03 · REPLICATE

Inspect the failures

Use sham/no-participant runs, leak canaries, independent software, blinded analysis and a held-out replication with locked parameters.

Research background: Mossbridge et al. (2012), Mossbridge et al. (2014), expectation-effects investigation, and Transparent Psi Project.
