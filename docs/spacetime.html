02 / SCHWARZSCHILD GEOMETRY

The horizon is not a curvature singularity.

Use geometrized units G=c=M=1. A stationary clock outside the horizon runs at √(1−2/r) relative to infinity. In ingoing Painlevé–Gullstrand coordinates radial light speeds are −√(2/r)±1, regular at r=2.

The null directions are coordinate slopes, not locally measured speeds. The redshift is for static emission received at infinity; static observers do not exist at or inside the horizon. K=48/r⁶ in M=1 units is finite at r=2 and divergent at r=0.

04 / EFFECTIVE POTENTIAL

Find the last stable circular orbit.

For a unit-mass timelike geodesic, ṙ²+V(r)=E² and V=(1−2/r)(1+L²/r²). Circular orbits exist for r>3M; stability changes at r=6M, the innermost stable circular orbit (ISCO).
