RESEARCH 005 / ARITHMETIC GEOMETRY

What survives
over a finite field?

Replace the complex numbers with a quadratic finite field extension. The circle becomes a norm-one torus, the sphere a Hermitian quadric, and the projective base a countable arithmetic variety. Every fiber can be checked exactly.

Enter the arithmetic laboratory ↓

An algebraic analogue of Hopf geometry, not a discretization of physical spacetime or a new proof of the Riemann hypothesis.

Count the geometry.

18 / EXTENSION COUNTS & ZETA FUNCTION

From geometry to arithmetic.

Increase the Hermitian dimension m and extension degree r. Over odd-degree extensions the torus is nonsplit; over even-degree extensions it splits. The point counts alternate accordingly, and determine an explicit rational zeta function.

PROOF / 005

What the experiment actually establishes.

005-A · EXACT

Free norm-one action

Every unit vector has trivial stabilizer under scalar multiplication. Every admissible line has p+1 unit vectors.

005-B · EXACT

Extension parity

The nonsplit torus becomes split over even extensions. Counts follow from the Hermitian norm equation or the split bilinear equation.

005-C · EXACT

Rational zeta function

The count sequence is a finite power sum, so its zeta function is an explicit product of reciprocal linear factors.
