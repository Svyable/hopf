02 / FRACTIONS INTO INTEGERS

Quarks. Nuclei. Ions.

Within the valence-quark description, a proton is uud and a neutron is udd. Up quarks carry +2/3 e; down quarks carry −1/3 e. Count their charges, then subtract the electrons. The charge identity is exact; the picture is not a spatial model of QCD.

03 / HOPF GEOMETRY MEETS EXPERIMENT

A quantum state is not a hidden arrow.

A normalized pure spin-½ state is a spinor on S³. Its common phase is unobservable in an isolated state; its ray lies on the Bloch sphere. Change the state and measurement axis to see the Born-rule probabilities.

State vector (cyan), analyzer (gold). The arrows encode abstract state and measurement directions.

Rotate common phase: the spinor moves in its Hopf fiber but probabilities remain invariant. An ideal projective spin measurement is a physical use of this geometry, not proof of a universal Hopf field theory.

Not planetary orbits.
