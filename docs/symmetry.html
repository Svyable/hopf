12 / CHARGE LATTICE

Consistency is not uniqueness.

Impose ordinary single-Higgs Yukawa charge relations and the SU(2)²U(1) anomaly. Two integer parameters remain: quark-doublet charge q and Higgs charge h. Explore what the sterile neutrino changes.

Q=q, uᶜ=−q−h, dᶜ=−q+h, L=−3q, eᶜ=3q+h, Nᶜ=3q−h.

Cyan: anomaly-free; gold outline: compatible with standard ℤ₆ quotient including Higgs. Grey: anomalous. Click or use sliders to select.
