Can perfect symmetry yield three?

Extend the single line-bundle obstruction to irreducible homogeneous holomorphic bundles on CP⁴. Borel–Weil–Bott reduces their index to a signed representation dimension.

09 / DIMENSIONAL COMPARISON

Three appears earlier.

Compare the canonical line-bundle index χ(CPⁿ,O(k)) across the four nested projective spaces. Index three first appears on CP¹ at k=2, and on CP² at k=1; it cannot occur on CP³ or CP⁴ for any integer twist.

INTERPRETATION CP² at k=1 gives a three-dimensional SU(3) representation (up to dual convention), not three symmetry-singlet copies. A triplet must not be confused with three generations.

002-A · DERIVED

Homogeneous obstruction

A single irreducible homogeneous holomorphic bundle on CP⁴ cannot have absolute index three, by Borel–Weil–Bott and the minimum nontrivial SU(5) dimension.

002-B · DERIVED

Dimensional distinction

Line twists produce absolute index three in CP¹ and CP², not CP³ or CP⁴. The resulting representation matters.
