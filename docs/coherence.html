04 / EVIDENCE & FALSIFICATION

What would count as evidence?

ESTABLISHED

Neural oscillations

EEG phase synchronization is measurable; common stimuli and artifacts can induce apparent coupling. Burgess (2013).

EXACT MODEL

Projective phase geometry

The Hopf quotient and collective order identity are mathematical; they do not make classical brainwaves quantum states.

SPECULATIVE

Collective consciousness

Hoffman's conscious-agent ontology and Sheldrake's morphic resonance remain unverified physical hypotheses. Coherence alone cannot identify them.

Open empirical path

Preregister bands, channels, time windows and one primary outcome; preserve trials and dyads in permutation tests; use event-matched pseudo-pairs, stimulus regressors and artifact checks; reserve a held-out replication. Start with public dual-EEG joint agency data or triadic EEG data. These datasets test ordinary social coupling, not nonlocal consciousness.
