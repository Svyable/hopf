17 / THE FIBRATION IN THREE DIMENSIONS

Every fiber links every other.

Pick a point on the base sphere and its whole fiber, a great circle of S³, appears in stereographic ℝ³. Pin several: any two fibers form a Hopf link. Fibers over one latitude sweep out a torus, and the colatitudes nest those tori around a single circle and a single line.

18 / HOPF INVARIANT LAB

Linking counts the map.

For a map f: S³ → S², the preimages of two distinct regular values are closed oriented curves whose linking number is the Hopf invariant H(f). Compose the Hopf map h with g(z₁,z₂) = (z₁ᵖ, z₂^q)/|·|, a map of degree pq, and the preimages become torus knots that link pq times.

H(h∘g) = deg(g)·H(h) for any g: S³ → S³, and the Hopf map h, with H(h) = 1 in this orientation, generates π₃(S²) ≅ ℤ. Adams (1960) proved that maps S²ⁿ⁻¹ → Sⁿ of Hopf invariant one exist only for n = 2, 4, 8: the complex, quaternionic and octonionic Hopf fibrations S³ → S², S⁷ → S⁴ and S¹⁵ → S⁸.

RESEARCH FRONTIER

Questions worth solving.

Keep established results, candidate constructions and physical hypotheses separate. These experiments supply reproducible tools, not proof of TUFT.

004-A · EXACT

Horizontal transport

The phase of a latitude lift equals minus half the enclosed solid angle for the stated orientation.

004-B · DERIVED

Gauge compensation

Section winding cancels exactly against the compensating phase along the loop.

004-C · NUMERICAL

Discrete holonomy

The overlap polygon approaches the continuous holonomy with O(N⁻²) error for fixed latitude.

004-D · EXACT

Linked fibers

Any two distinct Hopf fibers form a Hopf link, linking number +1 for the circle-action orientation, measured here by exact polygon crossings.

004-E · DERIVED

Villarceau tori

Fibers over colatitude θ fill a torus with R = sec(θ/2), r = tan(θ/2), so R² − r² = 1; each fiber is a Villarceau circle of radius R.

004-F · ESTABLISHED

Hopf invariant

[z₁ᵖ : z₂^q] has Hopf invariant pq; preimages are gcd(p,q) parallel torus knots. Invariant one occurs only in dimensions 2, 4, 8 (Adams).
