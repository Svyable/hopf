01 / GAUSSIAN PRIME FORGE

One energy. Thousands of sectors.

At a=1 (t=1/√2), let P be the product of r distinct primes congruent to 1 mod 4. Every decomposition of P into two squares gives an admissible Hopf sector at λ=16P−16. There are exactly 2r−1 unsigned sectors, by unique factorization in the Gaussian integers.

03 / LANDAU–RAMANUJAN EXPERIMENT

Most candidate energies are missing.

At a=1 let N=λ+16. Count distinct admissible eigenvalues D(N), and separately the total unsigned sectors S(N). An arithmetic density theorem and a lattice area calculation imply the mean occupancy grows as π/(8K)√log N, where K is the Landau–Ramanujan constant.

Prime factorization controls degeneracy
