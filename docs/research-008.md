# Research 008 — Numbers become matter

This atlas grounds the mathematical observatory in established quantum and atomic physics. It does not claim that TUFT derives these observations, or attribute a physical theory to Kurt Gödel. Proof, prediction and measurement are separate epistemic categories.

## A. Integers and electric charge
An element is defined by atomic number Z. Mass number A=Z+N counts nucleons, not atomic mass. The ionic charge in units of the positive elementary charge is Z−E, where E is electron count. For net valence content of Z protons and N neutrons, the up and down counts are U=2Z+N and D=Z+2N. Then (2U−D)/3=Z. Including E electrons gives (2U−D−3E)/3=Z−E. This is exact arithmetic given the established charge assignments. Quark labels are valence flavor counts, not a claim that a nucleon contains only three static particles. QCD includes gluons and sea quarks; nuclear binding and atomic binding do not follow from the count. Presets are selected familiar reference compositions, not a complete isotope or ion stability database. Other compositions are explicitly marked formal.

## B. Spinors and the Born rule
A pure spin-1/2 state z=(cos(θ/2), exp(iφ)sin(θ/2)) up to common phase gives a Bloch vector r=(sinθ cosφ,sinθ sinφ,cosθ). For measurement along a unit vector a, P(+)= (1+r·a)/2 and P(−)=1−P(+). Common phase changes spinor coordinates without changing the ray or measurement probabilities. This is the Hopf map S³→CP¹≅S² in an actual quantum-mechanical setting. It does not establish that spacetime itself is this bundle. Compare Research 004 and the Fiber Studio.

## C. One-electron orbitals
For a point nucleus with infinite mass and no relativistic terms, in units of the Bohr radius, the normalized real hydrogenic amplitudes are ψ1s=Z^(3/2) exp(−Zr)/sqrt(π), ψ2s=Z^(3/2)(2−Zr) exp(−Zr/2)/(4sqrt(2π)), and ψ2p=Z^(3/2) Zy exp(−Zr/2)/(4sqrt(2π)), with y the chosen orientation. These are three-dimensional amplitudes evaluated on a central slice, in units a0^(-3/2). Density is |ψ|². The display normalizes image brightness per view; it is not a calibrated measurement. The profile retains physical scaling in atomic units. The n shell has n² spatial orbital labels and 2n² spin-orbital labels, because the sum of 2l+1 for l=0…n−1 is n². This counting does not give the ground-state filling order of multi-electron atoms.

## D. Spectral comparison
The nonrelativistic infinite-mass model has E_n=−(hcR∞)Z²/n². Using CODATA 2022 hcR∞ ≈13.605693122990 eV and hc≈1239.841984332 eV nm, transitions have ΔE=E_upper−E_lower>0 and vacuum λ=hc/ΔE. The UI shows possible energy differences, not allowed-transition intensities. The NIST ASD reference ground-state ionization energies (eV) are H I 13.598434599702, He II 54.4177655282, Li III 122.45435913, including critically evaluated theoretical values; do not describe all three as direct experimental measurements. Residuals are shown rather than fitted away. Reduced nuclear mass, relativity, radiative and finite nuclear size corrections are omitted. NIST tabulates resolved spectral components and distinguishes air and vacuum wavelengths. Do not compare air wavelengths directly to these vacuum values.
