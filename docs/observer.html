02 / OBSERVER QUOTIENT

When does a description become a law?

Put a uniform probability on enabled moves (terminal state is absorbing). An observation partitions the world into blocks. A compressed observer has autonomous one-step dynamics for every initial microstate exactly when the partition is strongly lumpable.

Extension cardinality is a finite-world analogue of constraint weakness, not Bennett's complete Stack Theory. A larger extension does not by itself imply better prediction. The transition policy is a modeling choice, not a probability law of nature.

03 / EXACT COMBINATORICS

Compress the universe without simulating every history.

Each event moves one particular 1 past one particular 0. The prerequisites form a rectangular grid poset, and complete histories are its linear extensions. The hook-length formula gives an independent exact count.
